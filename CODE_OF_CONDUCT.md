# J.A.R.V.I.S. Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in the J.A.R.V.I.S. community a
welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience level, or personal
characteristics.

Everyone participating in this project is expected to act in ways that support
an open, constructive, and healthy community.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Treating other people with respect and empathy.
- Giving and accepting constructive technical feedback.
- Focusing criticism on ideas and code rather than individuals.
- Acknowledging mistakes, correcting them, and learning from them.
- Respecting privacy, security, and responsible disclosure requirements.
- Helping keep discussions relevant and productive.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory comments.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling, or deliberately disruptive conduct.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, private logs, voice data, or other sensitive material.
- Encouraging unsafe use of J.A.R.V.I.S. or bypassing its security controls.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional open-source community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull
requests, discussions, commits, reviews, and other communication made while
representing the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[security reporting channel](https://github.com/SrDarkoll/JARVIS/security/advisories/new)
and prefix the report title with `[Conduct]`.

Do not include sensitive incident details in a public issue. If private
reporting is unavailable, open a minimal issue asking the maintainer for a
private contact method without describing the incident publicly.

Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect the reporter's privacy as far
as reasonably possible, and avoid conflicts of interest when assigning a
reviewer.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or
other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Depending on the
severity and history of the behavior, consequences may include:

1. A private correction.
2. A formal warning.
3. A temporary restriction from project spaces.
4. A permanent ban from the community.

Enforcement decisions will be based on the impact of the behavior and will be
communicated privately when appropriate.
